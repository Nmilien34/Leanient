Onboarding v2, the conversion flow. The arc: a dark, intimate conversation (typewriter lines, each character landing with a soft haptic tick, previous lines dimming like a dialogue) that makes the prospect feel known, not alone, and standing on research (every stat carries its citation). Then the lights come on: their personalized plan, the hard paywall (no skip), and the review ask at peak excitement, right after purchase, before the app opens. Dark screens use the marketing deep-wash; light screens are the app's paper. Data collected is unchanged from the shipped flow, only the feel and order change.

THE CONVERSATION RULES (what keeps chip screens feeling alive): 1. Nothing is pre-rendered: the dim line types first, then the question with haptic ticks, and only then do the chips stagger in one by one. 2. Answers are spoken: tapping a chip dissolves the others and the pick glides up as a sent message (frame 3b). 3. The coach types back: a three-dot bubble, then the acknowledgment, then the next screen. 4. Single-select questions auto-advance after the acknowledgment beat; Continue exists only for multi-select and sliders. 5. Haptics grammar: soft tick per typed character, warm tap on select, one thump on acknowledgment. 6. Acknowledgments respond instantly (the coach was listening); next questions take a beat (the coach is thinking).
